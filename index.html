How the studio works

One person, start to finish

The same person designs, builds, writes and supports every app. When you email the studio, the person who made the app reads it.

Your data stays with you

Nursebook collects no data and needs no account. In Masri, your recordings and saved phrases stay on your device.

Sources are credited

Nursebook's textbook is Open RN's Nursing Fundamentals 2e, used under CC BY 4.0 and credited in the app. The practice questions are original.
