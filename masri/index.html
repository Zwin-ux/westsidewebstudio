Masri
iPhone & iPad

Speak the Arabic your family speaks.

Masri teaches Egyptian Arabic, the everyday Arabic of home and relatives. It's the language for greeting grandparents, saying thank you at dinner and wishing everyone a happy Eid. It isn't Modern Standard Arabic or Quranic Arabic.

What's inside

  • 24 short lessons built around real moments: greetings, family, food, the market and getting around.
  • 120 Egyptian phrases, each with audio voiced by Ahmed and Mona. These are synthetic voices.
  • My Phrases: save the words your family really uses and record them in a family member's voice.
  • Say it back: record yourself after the audio and hear the two one after the other.
  • Talk with Ahmed: short scripted conversations, including family scenes at Teta's door, at the dinner table and on an Eid visit.
  • A daily review that brings words back just before you'd forget them.
  • Arabic script, easy transliteration and English side by side.

Made for families

  • A parent or guardian can set up the account. Learners go by a nickname.
  • On accounts a parent sets up, buying, restoring, signing out and deleting the account ask for a quick parent check first.
  • There's no messaging with other people. Every conversation is scripted.
  • Your recordings and My Phrases stay on your device.

Free and Pro

The free plan includes the first 5 lessons, 20 reviews a day, up to 25 saved phrases and Say it back. Masri Pro is a subscription that unlocks all 24 lessons, unlimited reviews and saved phrases, and every Talk with Ahmed scene. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
