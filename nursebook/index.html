NurseBook Study
iPhone & iPad

Entrance exam prep and your Fundamentals textbook, in one offline app.

Nursebook is for nursing students and people applying to nursing school. Practise for the TEAS 7 and HESI A2, then read and annotate the textbook you'll use in your first semester.

Exam practice

  • 566 original practice questions in reading, math, English and grammar, vocabulary, anatomy and physiology, biology, chemistry and scientific reasoning.
  • Every question has a written explanation.
  • Free 10-question drills in every subject.
  • Timed TEAS 7 sections at the published question counts and time limits, and HESI A2 sections with pacing guides.
  • Missed questions come back until you get each one right twice in a row.

The textbook

  • The full Nursing Fundamentals 2e from Open RN, formatted for iPhone and iPad and available offline.
  • Search the whole book, tap key terms for definitions and study the glossary as flashcards.
  • Highlight, take notes and sketch with Apple Pencil. Scratchpads hold clips from the book that jump back to the page they came from.

Pricing

Nursebook is a free download. Subject drills and chapters 1 and 2 of the book are free. Timed exam sections and the full textbook are two separate one-time unlocks at $14.99 each. There's no subscription and no ads.

The textbook is also free to read online under its CC BY 4.0 license. The unlock pays for the app: the reader, search, notes and study tools.
